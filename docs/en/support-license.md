# Support and license

## Support

Email **hyunter@flycat.co.kr** for product support. To get a quick answer, include:

- your Unity version and the package version,
- what you did, step by step, and what you expected,
- the full Console message, if there is one,
- for a gameplay problem: the hero, the seed and the ascension level from [Play Preview](playtesting/play-preview.md), and the saved log (**Save log**) if you have one.

Do not send your whole project unless asked. A small project or content pack that shows the problem is enough.

## License

The package is licensed to you under the Unity Asset Store End User License Agreement.

Third-party components in the package, such as fonts, and the sources of the default sounds and music, are listed with their licenses in `Third-Party Notices.txt` in `Assets/DeckForge`. The full license texts are next to the files they cover.

Some default art and sounds were created with AI tools.
